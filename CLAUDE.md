# Luma Vitae - Brand Identity & Development Guide

## Brand Overview
**Name:** Luma Vitae (stylized lowercase: "luma vitae.")
**Full Name:** Luma Vitae
**Studio Name:** Luma Pilates Studio
**Location:** Panama City, Panama
**Language:** Spanish (primary), English (secondary)
**Tagline:** [pilates] [solace] [you]
**Philosophy:** "Not just fitness — a place for your soul to rest."

## Brand Essence
Luma is a female-focused luxury pilates studio. The brand embodies quiet feminine strength, inner calm, and self-care as a lifestyle. The studio is a sanctuary where time slows down and women reconnect with themselves.

**Core Quote:** "Female strength is always quiet."

## Typography
- **Logo/Brand:** Rounded sans-serif, all lowercase (Quicksand 400-600 or custom)
- **Headings:** Rounded sans-serif lowercase (Quicksand 300-500)
- **Body/Elegant:** Serif for luxury feel (Cormorant Garamond 300-400)
- **Accents:** Thin weight brackets style: [text here]

## Logo
- **Wordmark:** "luma" in rounded lowercase with distinctive dot/period
- **Icon:** Butterfly-like symbol made of four connected loops (two mirrored infinity symbols stacked vertically)
- **Usage:** Wordmark on cream backgrounds in rose; icon on rose backgrounds in cream

## Design Elements
- Wavy/scalloped borders (from welcome card motif)
- Generous whitespace and breathing room
- Soft organic curves, arched forms
- Warm, spa-like atmosphere
- Blurred photography backgrounds with brand overlays

## Voice & Tone
- Warm, inviting, intimate
- Spanish primary, English available
- Lowercase preference in design elements
- Bracket notation for keywords: [pilates] [solace] [you]
- Speaks directly to women seeking calm and strength
