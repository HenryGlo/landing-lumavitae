# Lumavitae landing design

The maintained brand and runtime tokens live in CLAUDE.md and the public landing
HTML. Event registration reuses comunidad-fundadora.html: Quicksand headings,
Cormorant body text, cream/rose palette, field groups and native controls.
Pilates Mon has its own page and no founder discounts or package selection.
Required fields follow the supplied screenshots; optional wellbeing notes remain
optional. The source question is “¿Cómo supiste del evento?” with Instagram,
TikTok, Recomendación and Otro. Policies and risk acceptance stay separate.

Submission goes only to Activepieces; no CRM secret appears in browser code.
Do not show success unless the CRM-backed response confirms it. Preserve inputs
on errors and timeouts, prevent duplicate submissions, focus errors/success and
respect reduced motion. Registration does not promise a paid or reserved place.
Event dates, booking URL and opening state belong to configurable CRM records.

Date fields and country Select/Listbox controls are native. The operating system
owns their popup geometry, keyboard interactions and localization. Branded
borders and the country label remain HTML/CSS around those native owners.
Notes auto-grow up to 320px and then scroll; manual resizing stays disabled.
